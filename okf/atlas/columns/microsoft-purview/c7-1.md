## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

The unified audit log captures AI interactions by user and agent with prompt and response message IDs, accessed resources, plugins, model provider and policy outcomes. Agent 365 audit covers agent-to-human, agent-to-tool and agent-to-agent interactions. Audit Premium retains records up to one year, or ten years with an add-on.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Purview Audit (Standard and Premium), DSPM activity explorer

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Documentation does not claim tamper-proof storage. Fabric data agent records exclude internal orchestration and reasoning. Third-party AI app audit needs pay-as-you-go billing.

## Options
