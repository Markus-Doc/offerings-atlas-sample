## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

Audit records flag jailbreak and cross prompt injection detections and can be sent to a SIEM through the Office 365 Management Activity API. Insider Risk Risky AI usage detects prompt injection and protected material access, feeds Defender XDR, and pseudonymises users by default.

## Products

Purview Audit, Insider Risk Management, Defender XDR integration

## Gaps

Purview is not a SIEM. Detection fields depend on the AI app populating them, and correlation and response ownership sit in Defender or Sentinel.
