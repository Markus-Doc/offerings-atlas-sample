## Answer

Evidence result: supported.

DSPM detects user visits and prompts to third-party generative AI sites categorised in the Defender for Cloud Apps catalog, such as ChatGPT, Gemini and DeepSeek, through the Purview browser extension and device onboarding. Network data security extends detection to apps and APIs through SASE integration.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

DSPM and DSPM for AI (classic), Purview browser extension, network data security

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Needs device onboarding and the browser extension, or a SASE partner. Coverage is limited to listed supported AI sites.
