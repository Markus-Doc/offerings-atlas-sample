## Answer

Evidence result: supported.

Retention policies retain, delete or both for prompts and responses across Microsoft Copilot experiences, enterprise AI apps including Foundry and ChatGPT Enterprise, and other AI apps. eDiscovery can search, hold and delete AI interaction data.

## Products

Data Lifecycle Management retention policies, eDiscovery

## Gaps

Deletion can take up to about 16 days. Non-Microsoft app content needs a collection policy. Residency is not addressed on these pages.

## Options
