## Answer

Evidence result: supported.

Agents and AI functions name a model or use auto, and SHOW CORTEX BASE MODELS reports lifecycle status, legacy and end-of-life dates. CORTEX_ENABLED_CROSS_REGION sets where inference may run, payloads are processed transiently and not persisted in the processing region, and context window limits are documented per model and region.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Cortex AI Functions, Cortex Agents, cross-region inference

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

With auto, the model chosen can change. Rate limits are not documented for AI functions, and Cortex Analyst picks models from a ranked list the customer cannot set directly.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Pin models in production agents, set CORTEX_ENABLED_CROSS_REGION explicitly, and restrict models with RBAC.
