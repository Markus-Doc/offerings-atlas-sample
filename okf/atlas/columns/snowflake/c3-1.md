How [Snowflake](profile.md) addresses the [Model Gateway](../../controls/c3-1.md) control. Researcher verdict: supported. Verification state: unverified. Agents may retrieve this page on the owner's acceptance of 2026-10-10, a proof-of-concept rule. No evaluator has checked it.

<!-- chunk: answer -->
## Answer

Evidence result: supported.

Model-level RBAC in SNOWFLAKE.MODELS grants per-model application roles to account roles, replacing the CORTEX_MODELS_ALLOWLIST parameter. Per-user quotas can block AI functions, Cortex Agents and CoWork when a user reaches a credit limit, resource budgets track spend per agent, and an orchestration budget caps each run. Cortex AI Guardrails screen for prompt injection and jailbreaks on Cortex Agents.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Model RBAC, per-user quotas, resource budgets, Cortex AI Guardrails

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Quota enforcement runs within minutes, so users can overshoot. Request rate limits are not configurable in the pages read. Guardrails are account-level, need Enterprise Edition, and do not cover AI functions.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Revoke CORTEX-MODEL-ROLE-ALL from PUBLIC, set the allowlist to None, enable per-user quota blocking and enable guardrails.
