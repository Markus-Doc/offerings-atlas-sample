## Answer

Evidence result: supported.

Elastic Security is a SIEM that ingests Bedrock model invocation logs, maps them to gen_ai fields and runs detection rules from the aws_bedrock rules folder. Attack Discovery groups alerts into attacks with users, hosts and MITRE ATT&CK mapping. The AI Assistant can anonymise fields sent to the LLM.

## Products

Elastic Security, AWS Bedrock integration, Attack Discovery

## Gaps

Prebuilt LLM rules focus on Bedrock. Coverage of other providers depends on custom ingestion. The Security Labs source is a 2024 blog.
