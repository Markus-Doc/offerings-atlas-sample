## Answer

NIM containers are digitally signed, ship SBOM and VEX records and are scanned during development, publication and continuously after release. The blog states no critical or high CVEs are allowed in published containers without VEX. Production branches get monthly security patches, and Government Ready STIG and FIPS containers are offered.
