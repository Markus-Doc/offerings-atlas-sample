## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

Logging callbacks send a standard payload with call ID, key, user and team to OpenTelemetry, Datadog, S3 and others. Message logging can be turned off or redacted per request, and key details can be redacted. Guardrail results and MCP tool drift alerts are emitted.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Logging callbacks, Alerting, Guardrail metrics

## Gaps
