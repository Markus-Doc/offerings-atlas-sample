## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

Audit logs (Enterprise) record create, update, delete and regenerate actions on keys, teams, users and models with actor, hashed key, before and after values, and can be exported to S3. Spend logs record each request with key, user and team, and can store prompts and responses. The docs make no tamper-resistance claim.

## Products

Audit Logs (Enterprise), Spend Logs

## Gaps
