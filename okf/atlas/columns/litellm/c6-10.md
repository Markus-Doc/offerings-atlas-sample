## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

Spend log retention is set by maximum_spend_logs_retention_period with a cleanup job, without an Enterprise licence. Message logging can be turned off or redacted, and Presidio can mask logged data only. region_name filtering limits routing to a region.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Spend Logs Deletion, Logging redaction, Router region filtering

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

With no retention setting nothing is deleted. Retention at providers and logging destinations is outside LiteLLM.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set retention, turn off message logging where not needed, and align callback destination retention.
