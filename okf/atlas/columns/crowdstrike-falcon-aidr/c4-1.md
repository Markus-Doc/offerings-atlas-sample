## Answer

The Agentic Identity Provider and Continuous Identity for AI Agents releases describe a cryptographically verifiable identity for each agent based on SPIFFE, registered automatically when Falcon Guardian discovers the agent, with actions tied to an accountable human or workload. AIDR itself only records app_id, user_id and collector metadata supplied by the caller.
