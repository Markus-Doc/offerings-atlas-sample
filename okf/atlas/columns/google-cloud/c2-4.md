## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

Sessions store events and temporary state for a conversation, can be retrieved to resume a paused conversation and deleted when finished. IAM Conditions on the sessionUserId attribute with sessionViewer, sessionEditor and sessionUser roles restrict access per user.
