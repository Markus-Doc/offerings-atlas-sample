## Answer

Evidence result: supported.

Models are called by explicit versioned IDs, and the lifecycle tables list release and retirement dates with at least 45 days to migrate. Quotas are documented per project and region. Request-response logging to BigQuery is opt-in, prompt logging for partner and open models is off by default, and data residency, CMEK, VPC-SC and Access Transparency apply to GA models.
