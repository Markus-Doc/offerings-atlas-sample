## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

Inference Providers acts as a proxy router with fastest, cheapest or preferred provider policies. Team and Enterprise admins can set a spending limit and disable providers. Enterprise resource groups add per-group monthly spend limits and can restrict who may bill Inference Providers to the organisation.

## Products

Inference Providers router, organisation provider settings, Resource Group spend limits and granular feature access
