## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

Every repository file under 2 GB is scanned with ClamAV at each commit, pickle files have their imports listed with suspicious imports flagged, and Protect AI and JFrog scanners report on model files. Model cards record base_model lineage, training datasets and licence, and GPG-signed commits show as verified.
