## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

Managed agent sessions store conversation items in a Lakebase-backed session store keyed by actor_id and session_id, with resume, fork, undo and clear operations. Access is granted at the store level.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Managed agent sessions

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

actor_id does not restrict access, so any principal with store access sees all sessions. No retention or TTL is documented. Beta.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use one store per trust boundary and add scheduled deletion.
