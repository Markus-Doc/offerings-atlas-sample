<!-- chunk: answer -->
## Answer

Evidence result: supported.

Data stays in the environment's chosen geography, with EU Data Boundary support and an admin setting for cross-region data movement. Transcripts are deleted after 30 days in Dataverse by default and 28 days in Copilot Studio storage, memory after 28 days of inactivity, and Purview retention policies can retain user and agent text for zero days. Customer-managed keys are supported.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Geographic data residency, transcript retention, Purview retention policies, customer-managed keys

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Cross-geo and external models can process data outside the region. External threat detection providers may store data elsewhere.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Disable cross-region data movement and external models where residency is required.
