## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

Conversations are written to the Dataverse ConversationTranscript table after 30 minutes of inactivity and are deleted by a bulk delete job after 30 days by default. Copilot Studio storage keeps transcript data for up to 28 days. Viewing transcripts needs the Bot Transcript Viewer role that only admins grant, and admins can stop transcripts being saved.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Conversation transcripts, Dataverse retention job, transcript controls

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Access control and resumption of live session state are not documented beyond transcripts. Transcripts are not written for developer, Dataverse for Teams or Microsoft Copilot agents.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set the Dataverse bulk delete schedule to the required retention and restrict the transcript viewer role.
