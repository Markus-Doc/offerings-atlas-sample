## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

Wiz Code analyses AI logic and integrations in code to flag unsafe tool use and embedded credentials, and its IDE plugins, skills and hooks scan for secrets, IaC misconfigurations and vulnerable dependencies at save, pre-commit and pre-push. AI-SPM finds hosted MCP servers, scans their files and flags MCP endpoints exposed to the internet. Review and defence of CLIs, skills and extensions themselves are not described.
