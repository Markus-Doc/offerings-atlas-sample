## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

Wiz detects self-hosted PyTorch and TensorFlow models on VMs and in buckets and flags unsafe formats such as pickle files that run commands or connect out. The 2026 AI risk post says Wiz scans files of hosted models, agents and MCP servers for malicious payloads, and the AI-BOM lists models, frameworks and libraries. Lineage and compliance attestation are not described.

## Products

Wiz AI-SPM model scanning, AI-BOM

## Gaps
