## Answer

Evidence result: supported.

The AI inventory catalogues models, agents, AI services, MCP servers, tools, guardrails and identities across cloud, SaaS and custom code, grouped into Services. The AI-BOM records models, frameworks, dependencies and libraries. Inventory is discovered, not registered.
