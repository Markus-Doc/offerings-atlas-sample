## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

Traces carry user and session correlation and can be exported to S3 or Azure Blob every 20 minutes or longer for SIEM ingestion. SDK masking redacts fields before export. Langfuse provides no security detections or response workflow itself, and traces third-party guardrails rather than detecting attacks.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Blob storage export (Pro with Teams add-on, Enterprise, self-hosted), masking, alerts, guardrail observations

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No native SIEM connector or security detection content. Detection depends on evaluators and external guardrails.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Ship blob exports to the SIEM, build detections on guardrail and evaluator scores, and assign alert ownership in the SOC.
