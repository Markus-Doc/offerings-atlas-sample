## Answer

Evidence result: supported.

AI PII Sanitizer masks PII in requests and responses through a separate PII service, with placeholder or synthetic replacement and custom regex patterns. AI RAG Injector limits which retrieval collections a consumer or consumer group can reach, with per-collection allow and deny lists and metadata filters.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

AI PII Sanitizer, AI RAG Injector collection ACLs

## Gaps
