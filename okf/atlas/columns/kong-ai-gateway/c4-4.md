## Answer

Evidence result: supported.

AI Agent entities of type a2a proxy A2A traffic and require callers to authenticate through an AI Auth Strategy (key-auth or OIDC). ACLs allow or deny AI Consumers, consumer groups or authenticated groups before traffic reaches the upstream agent, and the gateway can sign upstream requests with AWS SigV4.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

AI Agent entity, AI Auth Strategy, access.acls

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Access is controlled at the agent level, not per skill. Upstream authentication beyond SigV4 is not described. The getting-started guide configures no authentication.
