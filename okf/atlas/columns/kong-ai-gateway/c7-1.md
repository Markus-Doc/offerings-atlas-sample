## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

AI Gateway logs record consumer, request and response payloads, tokens, cost, MCP tool calls and every MCP ACL allow or deny decision. Konnect platform audit logs record authentication, authorisation and API access and can carry ED25519 signatures for verification.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

AI Gateway audit log, Konnect audit logs

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

AI traffic logs have no documented signing or immutability. Konnect audit logs are kept 7 days. Payload logging is off by default and may expose sensitive data. No reasoning capture.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Forward logs to a WORM store or SIEM and verify Konnect signatures on ingest.
