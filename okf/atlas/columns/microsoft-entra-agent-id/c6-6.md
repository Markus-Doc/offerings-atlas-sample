## Answer

Evidence result: supported.

Every agent identity and blueprint requires at least one sponsor accountable for purpose and lifecycle, with optional technical owners and managers. Sponsorship transfers automatically to the sponsor's manager when the sponsor leaves, and lifecycle workflow templates notify cosponsors and managers.
