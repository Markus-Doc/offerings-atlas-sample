## Answer

Evidence result: partial.

The AI Trust Layer LLM gateway applies policies at tenant, group or user level before requests reach models. It enforces product and model allow lists, region routing, a prompt injection guardrail, harmful content and IP protection checks, and third-party guardrail vendors. Conversational agents have a per-user monthly usage limit.

## Products

AI Trust Layer, centralized guardrails
